## What I'm building

**[V-TALKS](https://github.com/hetanshigithub/V-TALKS-Android-Prototype)** is an Android app that walks international students through F-1, CPT, OPT, STEM OPT and EAD processes — the decisions where getting it wrong costs you your status. It has step-by-step guides with interactive checklists, a deadline tracker with local reminders, and a career roadmap that ties visa timing to internship and job planning.

I built it as a human-centered design research project at Montclair State, starting from semi-structured interviews with F-1 graduate students and reflexive thematic analysis rather than from a feature list. Two decisions came out of that research: the chatbot is **rule-based rather than an LLM**, because a hallucinated answer in a visa-compliance context is worse than no answer, and every response prompts the student to verify with their DSO. The architecture is **local-first** — no backend, all data on the device — because privacy was a theme in every interview.
